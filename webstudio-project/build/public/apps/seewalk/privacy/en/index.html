Our approach

SeeWalk (“the app”) is designed to never send your measurement data, videos, or personal information off your device. The app does not even have permission to access the internet (no INTERNET permission).

Information the app uses

Camera video
The app records video with your device’s camera to measure how you walk. The video is analyzed only on your device and is deleted automatically once the analysis is finished. Videos and analysis results are never sent to any external server.

Walking measurements and profiles
Measurement results such as walking speed, step tempo, and step length, as well as profile information (name, height, and birth month — all optional), are stored only in a database on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

Step data from Health Connect
The app reads step counts from Health Connect only if you explicitly allow it. Step counts are used only to show your steps in the app (step charts) and to estimate the distance you walked. Step data read from Health Connect is never sent off your device, shared, or sold. You can withdraw this permission at any time in your device’s Health Connect settings.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect location, contacts, device identifiers, or advertising IDs. There is no account registration, and you never need to provide your real name or email address.

Sharing with third parties

None. Data never leaves the app, except when you choose to send a result image yourself using the “share card” feature.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are handled entirely on your device and do not use any external service.

Language settings

The app is available in English, Japanese, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, and Brazilian Portuguese. Your language choice is stored by the Android system on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Medical disclaimer

This app is not a medical device and is not intended for diagnosis, treatment, or prevention. Displayed values may include errors depending on recording conditions.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will announce it on this page. If the English and Japanese versions differ, the Japanese version prevails.
