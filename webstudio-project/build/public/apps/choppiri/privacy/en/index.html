Our approach

Choppiri (“the app”) stores your habits, records and character only on your device. We run no servers, and your records are never sent to us or to anyone else. No account is needed.

What is stored on your device

Habits and records
Habit names, schedules and target amounts, your daily records, rest days, the history of size adjustments, and optional feeling ratings are kept in a database on your device.

Character
The name, look and care history of your character.

Settings
Language, the time the day changes, the first day of the week, reminder times and similar settings.

All of this is removed from your device when you uninstall the app.

What we do not collect

The app itself does not access your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising ID. It contains no analytics or advertising.

Purchases on Google Play (Choppiri Pro)

Choppiri Pro is sold through Google Play, which handles the payment. Neither the app nor we receive your payment details. To unlock Pro, the app only receives from Google Play, on your device, which products you own. To check and process purchases, the Google Play billing component built into the app (Google Play Billing Library) communicates with Google’s servers. Information handled in that communication is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Backups

Manual backup and CSV export
Only when you ask, the app writes a file to a place you choose (your device, Google Drive and so on).

Automatic backup (Pro)
Once a week the app saves a copy in its own storage on your device, keeping the last four.

Android backup
If Android backup is turned on in your phone’s settings, Android may save the app’s data to your Google account. This is an Android feature; we cannot see that data.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders and home screen widgets work on your device and use no outside service.

Sharing with third parties

None. Your habit records never leave the app unless you share a file you exported yourself.

Languages

The app is available in Japanese, English, Spanish, Portuguese and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page.
