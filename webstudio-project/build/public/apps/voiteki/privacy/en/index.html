Voiteki Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 · 日本語

This policy explains how the Android app “Voiteki” (the “App”) handles information. The App is a voice-input keyboard provided by Sur Communication Inc. (“we”). This is an English translation; if it differs from the Japanese version, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Our basic policy

We do not collect, store or transmit any personal information or anything you type.

  • The App contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting or similar purposes.
  • The App never communicates with any server operated by us.
  • No account or sign-up is needed.

2. What the App does

The App works as an Android input method (IME / keyboard). It captures audio only after you tap the microphone button, turns it into text, and inserts that text into the text box where your cursor is.

3. How audio is handled

Audio is captured only while the microphone is active after you tap it. Where the audio goes depends on the speech-recognition engine you have chosen.

SettingWhere audio is sentOur involvement
This phone’s built-in feature
(default)		 The speech-recognition feature built into your device. Depending on your device and its settings, audio may be sent to the servers of the provider, such as Google None. We never receive your audio
Groq Whisper
(optional, for advanced users)		 Servers of Groq, Inc. (api.groq.com) None. We never receive your audio

In either case, audio is used only to turn it into text. The App never saves audio files on your device.

  • For the device’s built-in speech recognition, the privacy policies of your device maker and the recognition provider (in most cases Google) apply.
  • Groq Whisper works only if you enter your own API key. In that case, the privacy policy and terms of Groq, Inc. apply.
  • Speech is recognized in the App’s display language (Japanese, English, Spanish, Portuguese or Chinese). The language choice does not change where audio is sent.

4. Text you type (characters visible to a keyboard)

Because the App works as an Android input method, it is technically able to access the text you put into text boxes.

The App does not record, store or transmit this text.

The text is looked at only at that moment, and only to:

  • Carry out “Delete one”, “Delete all”, “Undo” and “Copy” correctly
  • Insert the result in the right place (for example, adding a space between words)

The App keeps no typing history, learned dictionary or conversion history. The Japanese kana-kanji conversion dictionary is a fixed dictionary bundled with the App and is never changed by what you type. Voice input is turned off in sensitive fields such as password fields.

5. Information stored only on your device

The following settings are stored only on your device, in the App’s private storage:

  • Text tidy-up settings (on/off for filler removal, adding punctuation, and so on)
  • Where the microphone appears on the screen
  • Your choice of speech-recognition engine
  • Recently used emoji
  • The Groq API key you entered (only if you entered one)
  • The display language you chose (saved by Android on your device)

None of these are sent to us. They are excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, and are deleted when you uninstall the App.

7. Sharing with third parties

We never provide or sell any information about you to third parties. The only information the App sends to a third party is the audio sent to the speech-recognition engine described in section 3.

8. Children

The App is not aimed at any particular age group. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (we do not collect information at all).

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post it on this page with a new “last updated” date. Important changes will also be announced in the App’s release notes.
